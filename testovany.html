Immersive Experience

Oživlá Večeře

This immersive experience follows the journey of food not as an object, but as a witness of time. A journey that today spans millions of seconds and hundreds of kilometers — yet once began in absolute silence. Visuals and sound trace the emergence of matter from nothingness: the birth of stars, the formation of planets, and the eventual rise of human systems. Food becomes a silent connector between origins and futures — shaped by the same forces that formed stars, societies, and technologies.
